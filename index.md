# Perth Extended Zone Customer Playbook
{: .fs-9 }

Technical guidance for deploying workloads to the Azure Perth Extended Zone — serving Western Australia with low-latency, data-resident cloud services.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

**Version 1.2 — May 2025**
{: .label .label-green }

---

The forthcoming launch of the Perth Extended Zone represents a significant opportunity. By storing and processing your data within Western Australia, you can fulfil your data residency and compliance obligations, minimise workload latency, and achieve your sustainability goals.

This playbook offers specific guidance for the Perth Extended Zone, serving as an initial reference when utilizing an Azure Extended Zone. It focuses on three key areas:

- **[Overview of Azure Extended Zones]({% link docs/overview/index.md %})** — What Extended Zones are, deployment scenarios, service availability, SLAs, pricing, and onboarding.
- **[Design Considerations]({% link docs/design-considerations/index.md %})** — Resource organisation, management, governance, observability, networking, BCDR, and security.
- **[Use Cases]({% link docs/use-cases.md %})** — Real-world deployment patterns *(coming soon)*.

## Intended Audience

This technical document is intended for **solution architects** and other technical stakeholders as they plan their use of Azure Extended Zones.
