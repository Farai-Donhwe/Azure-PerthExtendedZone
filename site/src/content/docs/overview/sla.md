Service Level Agreements (SLAs) are Microsoft's commitment to provide a certain level of service quality, including uptime and connectivity, for their online services. These agreements capture the performance standards that customers can expect from Azure services and specify the compensation customers are entitled to if these standards are not met.

:::caution
Extended Zones are **single-zone locations**, and the below SLAs take this into consideration.
:::
